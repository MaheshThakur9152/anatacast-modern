02 — Core disciplines

01

Investment casting

Lost-wax casting in ferrous alloys for complex, near-net shapes with fine detail and excellent as-cast finish.

  • In-house wax tooling
  • Ceramic shell building
  • Induction melting
  • Spectro-verified chemistry

02

CNC machining

Castings move straight to our machine shop for critical faces, bores and threads — finished to drawing tolerance.

  • CNC turning
  • Vertical machining centres
  • 4/5-axis milling
  • Drilling & tapping

03

Heat treatment

Controlled thermal cycles deliver the hardness, strength and corrosion resistance your application demands.

  • Solution annealing
  • Normalising
  • Hardening & tempering
  • Case hardening

04

Testing & quality

Every lot is measured and documented. Reports travel with the parts, so your incoming inspection is simple.

  • CMM inspection
  • Hardness & tensile
  • Dye penetrant
  • Radiography · MTC
