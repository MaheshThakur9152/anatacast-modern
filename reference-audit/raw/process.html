The process

From waxto finished.

Lost-wax casting is a 5,000-year-old craft. We run it with modern tooling, controlled chemistry and in-house machining.

02 — The process

Six stages. One accountable partner.

01

Tooling & wax

Aluminium dies inject precise wax replicas of your part, assembled onto a sprue to form the casting tree.

Pattern accuracy ±0.05 mm

  1. Tooling & wax
  2. Shell building
  3. Melting & pouring
  4. Fettling & heat treatment
  5. CNC machining
  6. Inspection & dispatch

03 — Working with us

From enquiry to series production.

  1. 01

    Drawing review

    Our engineers study your 2D/3D data for castability, gating and machining allowance.

  2. 02

    Feasibility & quote

    You receive a feasibility note, suggested changes if any, and a clear quotation.

  3. 03

    Tooling

    Wax injection dies are built and the first patterns are checked against your model.

  4. 04

    First article

    Sample parts are cast, machined and fully inspected with a dimensional report for approval.

  5. 05

    Series production

    Approved process is locked. Every lot ships with material certificates and inspection data.
