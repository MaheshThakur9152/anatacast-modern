Ananta Cast is an investment casting company that thinks like a machine shop. We cast ferrous alloys, heat treat them, machine them and measure them — then ship them ready to assemble.

02 — The name

Ananta means endless.

In Sanskrit, ananta is the infinite — without end. It is how we think about our customers: not single orders, but partnerships that run for years, part after part, lot after lot.

Every shell is built by hand, dip by dip, before a single gram of steel is poured.
